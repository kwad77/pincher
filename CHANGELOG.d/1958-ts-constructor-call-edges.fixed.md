**TypeScript tree-sitter extraction now records constructor-invocation edges ([#1958](https://github.com/kwad77/pincher/issues/1958)).** The AST-tier TS extractor only emitted CALLS from `call_expression`, so `new Foo()` instantiations were dropped vs the regex tier (which counted them via its `name(` scan) — losing the "who instantiates this class" signal that `trace`/hotspots rely on. `new_expression` now emits a CALLS edge to the constructed type, keeping TS edge coverage at least as rich as the regex tier. (The node-monorepo corpus golden was also refreshed for the intended TS confidence 0.85→1.0 upgrade.)
