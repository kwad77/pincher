`pincher supervised` no longer closes the host MCP transport when respawn gives up (binary-swap window outlasting the retry budget, or the circuit breaker tripping): the supervisor now degrades — answering requests with JSON-RPC errors and retrying the spawn in the background — and recovers automatically; the status tool reports `degraded`/`degraded_reason`/`degraded_since`, and `PINCHER_SUPERVISED_EXIT_ON_GIVEUP=1` restores the old exit behavior (#1901)
