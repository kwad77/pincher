New `branch_overlap` MCP tool — merge-order risk between two in-flight branches ([#1856](https://github.com/kwad77/pincher/issues/1856)). `changes scope=base:` previews one branch's blast radius; `branch_overlap` composes the idea for a pair: it diffs `branch_a` and `branch_b` against their shared merge-base, maps the changed files to symbols, intersects the sets, and returns a `verdict` — independent (disjoint files), low risk (shared files but no shared symbols), or merge-order risk (shared symbols, the second branch to merge needs re-review). Deterministic: `git diff` + pincher's file→symbol index.
