**ADR-0008: AST-tier extraction via real tree-sitter compiled to WASM (pure-Go) ([#1689](https://github.com/kwad77/pincher/issues/1689)).** Proposed. Re-opens [ADR-0006](docs/adr/0006-non-go-ast-strategy.md)'s "regex sufficient" stance on the cost axis: a pure-Go path to real upstream tree-sitter now exists — grammars compiled to WebAssembly with `zig cc` (hermetic, not emscripten) and run via `wazero`, preserving the `CGO_ENABLED=0` single-static-binary invariant. **Measured** (EGDL spike): real-tree-sitter-via-WASM parsed 100% of the ripgrep Rust corpus at **0% parse errors and 7.6× faster** than a native-Go tree-sitter reimplementation (which failed on 15% of files). Decision splits into a *settled* engine choice (WASM, measured on Rust) and *gated* per-language promotion (Java/TS/C# remain unmeasured — Phase-1 corpus-gate task). An independent adversarial review hardened the ADR around concurrency/RSS, marshaling-bug risk (silently-wrong 1.0 edges), platform-varying parse quality, and reproducible WASM builds. Ships behind a per-file `HasError()`→regex dispatcher (zero regression) with edges stamped `EXTRACTED` + producing-engine provenance. Status Proposed until the Phase-1 anchors pass the corpus/throughput/RSS gates.
