`investigate_failure` no longer drops the exact crash-frame symbol from `implicated_symbols` ([#1831](https://github.com/kwad77/pincher/issues/1831)). The candidate pool was truncated to `maxSuspects*2` by raw BM25 *before* the #1751 exact-name score boost ran — so on a struct with many methods, loose matches on the receiver-type token (e.g. `Indexer`) could fill the cap and evict the function literally named in the panic frame. The pre-sort now ranks exact-frame-name matches ahead of loose BM25 hits so the crash site always survives into scoring.
