`pincher --help` groups its flags instead of dumping all 14 in one flat alphabetical list ([#1710](https://github.com/kwad77/pincher/issues/1710) v0.92). The no-subcommand flag list is now split into **Common** (`--version`, `--http`, `--http-key`, `--data-dir`, `--verbose` — the handful most users touch) and **Deployment & tuning** (the HTTP/reverse-proxy/pool knobs almost nobody sets by hand, each of which already has a `$PINCHER_*` env equivalent). Every flag still works and is still listed — the change is presentation only.
