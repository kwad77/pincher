`pincher export-graph` dumps a project's symbol + edge graph to JSON, GraphML, or DOT ([#1850](https://github.com/kwad77/pincher/issues/1850)). pincher's knowledge graph is deterministic and AST-derived; this exposes it to external graph tooling — Gephi, Cytoscape, yEd, Graphviz, Neo4j importers — without pincher having to build visualization itself. `--format` selects the encoding (json keeps every field and every edge; graphml/dot drop edges whose endpoints aren't both nodes), `--project` picks the project, `--out` writes to a file. Read-only, no schema change.
